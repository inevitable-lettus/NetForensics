## Bug
The C2-beacon detector flags benign SSDP (Simple Service Discovery Protocol) multicast as beaconing. In `ctu-185-1` (CTU Stratosphere, fetched by PR #15), a host sends to `239.255.255.250` every ~120 s with almost no jitter, so the coefficient of variation falls under `max_coefficient_of_variation` (0.1) and the group reaches `min_connections` (10). The evidence string is accurate (the traffic *is* regular) but the conclusion is a false positive: this is device discovery, not command and control.

## Why it matters
The tool's pitch is defensible evidence; a magistrate-facing finding on a router's discovery chatter is the false-positive example `docs/logic.md` already warns about ("Heartbeats / keep-alives"). `Allowlists.beacon_dst_ips` exists but is empty, and it is the wrong shape for this (a whole multicast range, not one address).

2. `analyze_pcap` on it; observe a `c2_beacon` finding with `dst_ip` `239.255.255.250`.

## Options (human decides; not for implementation until then)
1. Suppress destinations in multicast ranges (224.0.0.0/4 and ff00::/8) in the detector, with the rule stated in `docs/logic.md`.
2. Extend `Allowlists.beacon_dst_ips` to accept CIDR ranges and ship a default list (multicast, link-local broadcast), configured in `backend/config.py` only.
3. Keep the finding but mark it lower severity / add a "likely benign: multicast discovery" note in the evidence text, so the human analyst sees the context.
Recommendation: option 2 (config-driven, one source of truth, auditable), with the multicast ranges stated in the evidence-free "suppressed" log so suppression is not silent. Do not hardcode thresholds or ranges outside config.
